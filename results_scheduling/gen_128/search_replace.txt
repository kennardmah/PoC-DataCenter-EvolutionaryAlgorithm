<centralized_objective_and_co_location>
This change centralizes all objective function calculations (`compute_cost`, `delay_penalty`, `resilience_bonus`, `same_server_chain_bonus`, and `co_location_lookahead_bonus`) into a single `_calculate_operation_objective_value` function.

**Rationale for the change:**
1.  **Integrate Resilience Bonus**: The current program does not apply a resilience bonus based on remaining capacity, which was present in some higher-scoring prior versions. This change restores and integrates this logic, incentivizing scheduling on more robust machines, especially important in failure scenarios.
2.  **Co-location Incentive (New Feature)**: Introduces a `CO_LOCATION_LOOKAHEAD_BONUS` that incentivizes scheduling an operation on a server where the *next* operation of the same job can also be immediately scheduled. This promotes job locality, potentially reducing overheads and improving flow, especially for multi-operation jobs. The bonus is higher for critical/high priority jobs and applied selectively to local servers for lower priority jobs.
3.  **Centralized Logic**: Renames `same_server_bonus_cost_reduction` to `SAME_SERVER_CHAIN_BONUS` and integrates its application within the new comprehensive objective function. This ensures all factors contributing to an operation's objective value are computed in one place, making the objective function more transparent, flexible, and easier to tune.
4.  **Improved Decision-Making**: By consolidating all objective factors, the scheduler can make more informed trade-offs between immediate cost, future delay penalties, resilience (remaining capacity), and job flow continuity.
5.  **Refined Priority-Awareness**: The bonuses are dynamically weighted by job priority, aligning with the mission-critical nature of government data centers where critical jobs benefit more from resilience and continuous flow.

This refactoring sets up for a more robust and efficient scheduling mechanism by accurately modeling various cost and performance drivers.

The changes are broken down into multiple diffs for clarity and to avoid "No changes applied" errors due to mismatches.

        at a given start time. This includes compute cost, future delay penalty, resilience bonus,
        same-server chain bonus, and co-location lookahead bonus.

        op_compute_cost = 0.0
        for t in range(start_time, start_time + op_duration):

            op_compute_cost += self.inst.server_cost[server_id][cost_t]

        remaining_operations_duration = sum(o.duration for o in job_obj.operations[op_idx + 1:])

            )
