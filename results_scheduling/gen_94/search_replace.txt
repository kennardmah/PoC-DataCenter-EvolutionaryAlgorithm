<DESCRIPTION>
This enhancement dynamically adjusts the local machine search window based on the job's priority. High-priority jobs (e.g., CRITICAL, HIGH) will have a smaller lookahead window, favoring earlier completion to prevent delays, even if it means slightly higher costs. Low-priority jobs (e.g., MEDIUM, LOW) will have a wider search window, allowing the scheduler to explore further into the future for potentially cheaper off-peak slots, thereby optimizing compute cost more aggressively. This balances the trade-off between minimizing delay and minimizing cost according to job importance.

Additionally, refined tie-breaking logic is added. When multiple (server, start_time) combinations yield the exact same minimum objective cost, the scheduler will now prioritize the option that has the earliest start time. This ensures a deterministic and more efficient schedule by preferring to free up resources sooner when all other factors are equal.
