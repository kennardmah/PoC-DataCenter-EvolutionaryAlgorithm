<sort_local_servers_by_resilience>
Prioritize local servers during scheduling by sorting them based on their immediately available capacity. This heuristic aims to select "healthier" or less-degraded local machines first, especially beneficial in failure scenarios, to improve robustness and reduce the likelihood of subsequent cloud relocations or delays. The `_get_available_capacity` function, which already accounts for machine failures, is used to determine a server's immediate health.
