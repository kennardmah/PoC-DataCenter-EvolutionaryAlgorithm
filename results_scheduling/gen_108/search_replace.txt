<name>
precompute_capacities_and_refine_get_available
</name>

<description>
This change improves performance by precomputing the static available capacity for all local servers across the time horizon, considering only machine failures. This avoids redundant and expensive recalculations of exponential failure/recovery functions for every `_get_available_capacity` call.

The `_get_available_capacity` method is updated to:
1.  Return effectively infinite capacity for cloud servers immediately.
2.  Use the precomputed values for local servers when the time is within the `inst.time_horizon`.
3.  Fall back to the base capacity for times beyond the precomputed horizon, assuming failures resolve within `TIME_HORIZON` for practical purposes.

This change optimizes a frequently called function, which should lead to faster schedule generation, especially in scenarios with many jobs or operations, or complex failure patterns.
</description>
