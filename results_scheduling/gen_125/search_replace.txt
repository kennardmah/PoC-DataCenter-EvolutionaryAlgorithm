<resilience_bonus_in_objective>
This change re-integrates a resilience bonus directly into the operation objective function. Previously, a similar concept (`min_free_capacity`) was used only for tie-breaking. By making it a direct component of the objective value, the scheduler will proactively favor local servers that maintain a higher capacity buffer during an operation's execution, thereby enhancing robustness against unexpected spikes or further degradations, especially for critical jobs. The existing contention penalty logic has been removed as this resilience bonus covers similar ground but with a more positive "reward" rather than a "penalty". The tie-breaking tuple for server selection has been simplified to remove the redundant `neg_min_free_capacity` component, as resilience is now integrated into the primary `objective_cost`.

This modification directly addresses a refinement of "Recommendation 4: Integrate Operational Risk Factor into Server Selection and Objective" by explicitly valuing resilience in the primary decision metric. It aims to reduce cloud relocations and improve overall system stability in failure scenarios, while still optimizing for cost and delay.
