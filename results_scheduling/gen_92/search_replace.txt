<DESCRIPTION>
This improvement enhances the "Cost-Time Windows" strategy for LOW priority jobs.
Instead of using a fixed small lookahead window (`LOW_PRIORITY_LOCAL_SEARCH_WINDOW`), low-priority jobs now search for available local slots up to the latest possible start time that still allows the entire job to meet its deadline. This ensures that low-priority jobs can exploit cheaper off-peak hours later in the schedule, as long as it doesn't cause them to miss their deadline. This change aims to reduce compute costs for low-priority jobs by strategically delaying them when slack allows, without incurring delay penalties. High-priority jobs retain their preference for earlier scheduling.
</DESCRIPTION>
