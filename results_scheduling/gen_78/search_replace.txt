<select_best_local_capacity>
Refines local server selection by prioritizing earliest start, then lowest cost, and finally, the highest minimum available capacity throughout the operation's duration. This strategy aims to improve resilience by choosing servers that maintain a healthier capacity buffer, reducing the risk of future contention or hidden bottlenecks during the operation. The previous sorting of local servers by instantaneous available capacity is removed as the full candidate evaluation provides a more robust selection.
