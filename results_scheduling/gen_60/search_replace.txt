<same_server_chaining_bonus>
Apply a significant cost reduction (bonus) to critical and high-priority jobs if their operations can be scheduled on the same local machine as the preceding operation. This incentivizes chaining operations for critical workloads on the same server to minimize internal job delays, aligning with the robustness and priority objectives.

The `JobShopScheduler` class will be updated:
1.  A new instance variable `same_server_bonus_cost_reduction` will be introduced to quantify the bonus.
2.  In `_find_best_local_slot`, when evaluating local server candidates:
    *   If the current job is `CRITICAL` or `HIGH` priority (checked via `job.priority_score <= self.priority_map["HIGH"]`), AND
    *   The candidate server is the `last_scheduled_server` for this job,
    *   Then, the `effective_cost_candidate` will be reduced by `same_server_bonus_cost_reduction`. This makes that candidate appear cheaper in the sorting logic, thus prioritizing it.
3.  The `candidates` tuple in `_find_best_local_slot` will be updated to reflect this change, removing the now redundant `same_server_penalty` element and thus having one less element, requiring adjustment in the `return` statement index.

This change aims to improve the performance of high-priority jobs by ensuring their operations are tightly coupled, reducing overall completion time and thus delay penalties, particularly crucial in emergency scenarios.
