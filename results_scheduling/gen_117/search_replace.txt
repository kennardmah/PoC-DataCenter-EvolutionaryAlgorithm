<DESCRIPTION>
This modification introduces a "server affinity bonus" to the objective function. When an operation is scheduled, if it can be placed on the *same server* as the immediately preceding operation of the same job, a small cost reduction (SERVER_AFFINITY_BONUS) is applied. This incentivizes job locality, which can reduce implicit overheads such as data transfer and context switching between sequential operations of a job. The bonus acts as a tie-breaker or a slight preference in cases where other costs are similar, guiding the scheduler to group operations of a job on the same resource where efficient. This directly implements Recommendation 4 from the suggested improvements.
</DESCRIPTION>
