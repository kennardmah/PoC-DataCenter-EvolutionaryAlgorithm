<adaptive_emergency_prioritization>
This change introduces an adaptive job prioritization mechanism, specifically for emergency scenarios. If machine failures are present, CRITICAL and HIGH priority jobs are given an additional urgency bonus, effectively reducing their perceived slack by a fixed amount (e.g., 5 hours). This nudges the scheduler to prioritize these vital jobs more aggressively during times of crisis, aiming to minimize their delay penalties which are heavily weighted in the total cost. This directly implements the "Adaptive Job Prioritization Based on Overall System Load and Future Capacity Crunch" strategy by dynamically adjusting the sorting key based on the scenario state.
