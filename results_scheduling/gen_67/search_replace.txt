<stickyscheduling_prioritize_local>
Implements "Sticky Scheduling" by prioritizing the local server that was used for the immediately preceding operation of the same job. If that server is compatible and available, it is moved to the front of the `local_servers_for_type` list, giving it preferential treatment during the earliest-slot search. This promotes sequential operations of a job running on the same machine, potentially reducing overhead and improving throughput.
</DESCRIPTION>

<stickyscheduling_update_last_server>
