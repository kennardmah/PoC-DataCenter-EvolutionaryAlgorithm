<resilience_bonus_objective>
Integrates a "resilience bonus" into the operation's objective value, incentivizing placements on local servers that maintain higher minimum free capacity during operation execution. This directly supports the "Robustness" evaluation criterion, especially important in earthquake scenarios, by preferring more stable local resources. Critical jobs receive an amplified resilience bonus. The tie-breaking logic in `run_scheduling` is also refactored to use a comprehensive tuple, ensuring consistent multi-criteria selection (objective cost, start time, server type, resilience, server ID). A `RESILIENCE_BONUS_FACTOR` is added to `__init__` for tunable weighting. The `_calculate_operation_objective_value` function's signature remains unchanged, but its internal logic is extended to use the existing `_calculate_min_free_capacity_during_op` helper.
