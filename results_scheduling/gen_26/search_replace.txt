<DESCRIPTION>
This enhancement pre-computes the `total_duration` and `priority_score` for each job once at the beginning of the `construct_schedule` function. These values are stored directly on the `Job` objects.

The job prioritization logic (`sorted_jobs`) is then updated to leverage these pre-calculated properties. This ensures that:
1.  Calculations for total duration and priority mapping are not repeated within the sort key lambda function, improving efficiency.
2.  The job sorting is more explicit and directly uses the enriched `Job` object attributes, making the code cleaner and easier to understand.
3.  The multi-criteria prioritization remains effective, ordering jobs by priority level, then by slack (effective deadline), then by delay weight, and finally by arrival time and ID for tie-breaking.
</DESCRIPTION>
