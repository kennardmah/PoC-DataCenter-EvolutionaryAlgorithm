<adaptive_local_search_window>
This enhancement re-introduces an adaptive local machine search window based on job priority. For critical jobs, the scheduler will search a narrower window to prioritize immediate availability and rapid completion, minimizing potential delays. For lower-priority jobs, a wider window is explored, allowing the scheduler to find more cost-effective slots during off-peak hours or later times, balancing resource utilization and cost. This aligns with the "Cost-Time Windows" strategy and "Priority-Aware Ordering" to dynamically optimize resource allocation based on urgency.

The search window parameters (`BASE_LOCAL_SEARCH_WINDOW_MULTIPLIER` and `BASE_LOCAL_SEARCH_WINDOW_ADD`) are defined as class attributes, and `PRIORITY_SEARCH_WINDOW_FACTORS` maps job priority levels to multipliers for the base window, enabling dynamic adjustment. The `search_end_time` calculation now incorporates this `priority_factor`.
