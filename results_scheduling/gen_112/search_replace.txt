<NAME>
operation_objective_chaining_bonus
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Modifies `_calculate_operation_objective_value` to include the `job_id` in its signature and apply a chaining bonus. If the current operation is not the first one for its job (`op_idx > 0`), is being scheduled on a local machine, and that local machine is the same as where the *previous* operation of the same job was scheduled, a `CHAINING_BONUS_COST` is subtracted from its `op_compute_cost`. This incentivizes keeping sequential operations of a job on the same physical server to reduce perceived overhead.
</DESCRIPTION>
