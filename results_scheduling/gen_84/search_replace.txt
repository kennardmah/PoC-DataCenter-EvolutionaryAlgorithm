Adaptive Lookahead Window for Local Search

This change introduces an adaptive lookahead window when searching for available slots on local machines. Instead of searching the entire `time_horizon` for every operation, the search window is dynamically adjusted based on the job's priority.

Rationale:
1.  **Reduced Computational Overhead**: For lower priority jobs, searching the entire `time_horizon` for an optimal local slot can be computationally expensive and often unnecessary. A shorter lookahead is sufficient, allowing the scheduler to quickly determine if a local slot is *immediately* available, or if cloud resources should be considered sooner to maintain flow for these less critical tasks.
2.  **Strategic Resource Allocation**: High-priority jobs (CRITICAL, HIGH) still benefit from a wider lookahead window, enabling them to find the most cost-effective and earliest local placements, minimizing potential delays which are heavily penalized.
3.  **Improved Responsiveness**: Prevents lower priority jobs from "reserving" or implicitly blocking local capacity too far into the future, which might be better utilized by an urgent, higher-priority job that arrives later or becomes available earlier. This can lead to a more balanced and robust schedule, especially under load or during emergencies.

The lookahead window is calculated as a base window plus an additional duration specific to the job's priority, capped by the overall `time_horizon`.
