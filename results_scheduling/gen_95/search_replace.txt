<robust_tie_breaking>
This enhancement refactors the core operation scheduling logic to collect all viable local and cloud server placement options before making a final decision. Instead of a sequential 'best-so-far' update, it now evaluates each potential (server, start_time) combination and stores it with its objective cost and several tie-breaking metrics. After all candidates are gathered, they are rigorously sorted using a multi-criteria key:
1.  **Minimize total objective cost** (compute cost + delay penalty).
2.  **Prefer local over cloud** (to maximize efficient resource use).
3.  **Minimize start time** (to complete jobs sooner).
4.  **Maximize minimum free capacity** during the operation's execution (for robustness against further failures or contention).
5.  **Minimize server ID** (for deterministic tie-breaking).

This robust tie-breaking ensures that even when multiple options yield the same primary objective value, the scheduler makes a more strategically sound choice that considers system health, resource utilization, and overall resilience, particularly beneficial in dynamic, failure-prone environments.
