<intelligent_scheduler_refactor>
This refactor replaces the existing basic FIFO scheduling logic with a more sophisticated approach. It introduces:
1.  **Objective Function-Driven Placement**: Each operation's placement (server and start time) is chosen by minimizing a combined objective of compute cost and projected delay penalty, directly addressing the problem's evaluation criteria.
2.  **Extended Local Search Window**: Local machines are searched beyond the immediate earliest start time to find cheaper slots, balancing cost and urgency ("Cost-Time Windows"). The search window now dynamically adapts based on job priority.
3.  **Smart Local/Cloud Tradeoff**: Both local and cloud options are explicitly evaluated and compared using the objective function. Cloud is no longer a last-resort fallback but a strategic option, especially for high-priority jobs or when local alternatives are expensive or lead to significant delays.
4.  **Priority-Aware Scheduling**: The overall job ordering remains priority-aware, and the local search window also adapts to job priority.
5.  **Robustness Metric for Tie-breaking**: When objective costs are equal, the scheduler prefers local machines, earlier start times, and then servers with more remaining free capacity (for local machines) to enhance resilience and stability.

This significantly elevates the scheduling algorithm from a greedy online FIFO to a lookahead, cost-aware heuristic.
