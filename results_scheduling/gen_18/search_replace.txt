<add_job_dataclass_attributes>
Adds `total_duration` and `priority_score` attributes to the `Job` dataclass. These attributes will be pre-calculated once for each job during schedule construction, enabling more sophisticated prioritization heuristics based on a job's total length and its mapped priority level without re-calculating them repeatedly. This is a foundational change for implementing more intelligent job ordering and operation placement.
</DESCRIPTION>
