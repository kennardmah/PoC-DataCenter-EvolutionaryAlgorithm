The constants `BASE_LOCAL_SEARCH_WINDOW_HOURS`, `HIGH_PRIORITY_LOCAL_SEARCH_WINDOW_HOURS`, and `EMERGENCY_LOCAL_SEARCH_WINDOW_HOURS` will allow the scheduler to dynamically adjust its search depth for local slots based on job priority. `EMERGENCY_CLOUD_TRIGGER_DELAY_THRESHOLD` and `EMERGENCY_CLOUD_COST_REDUCTION_FACTOR` will enable a more nuanced cloud decision for critical jobs facing significant local delays.
