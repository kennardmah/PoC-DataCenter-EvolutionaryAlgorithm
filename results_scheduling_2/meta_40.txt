This will encourage the scheduler to prioritize local resources when feasible, mirroring the effective strategies of Generations 32-39 which all mention dynamic cloud deterrence.

3.  **Implement Enhanced Lookahead Simulation for Current Job's Remaining Operations**: Elevate the current `remaining_duration` calculation, which is a simple sum, to a more sophisticated simulation.
