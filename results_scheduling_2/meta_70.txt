# META RECOMMENDATIONS

The following are actionable recommendations for the next program generations:

Here are 5 actionable recommendations for future program mutations, building upon the identified successful patterns and aiming to enhance the current program:

1.  **Refine Job-Internal Lookahead with Limited Best-Fit for Subsequent Operations**: Enhance the "lookahead simulation for remaining job operations" (Recommendation 1 from previous list) by not just greedily placing subsequent operations. Instead, within that lookahead, perform a *limited "best-fit" search* for each future operation. This means evaluating a small window of potential start times and servers for each of the remaining operations to calculate a more accurate *projected total future cost* for the entire job, mirroring the depth of Generation 50's successful lookahead.

2.  **Implement Adaptive Local Search Window Based on Job Urgency**: While transitioning to a "Best Fit" bounded local search (Recommendation 2 from previous list), make the `LOCAL_SEARCH_WINDOW_HOURS` dynamic. For jobs with 'Critical' or 'High' priority, or those very close to their `deadline`, expand the search window to explore a wider range of local slots. This allows the scheduler to find a potentially better, though slightly later, local placement that avoids costly cloud usage or severe deadline violations for critical work.

3.  **Introduce Congestion-Responsive Dynamic Cloud Deterrence**: Beyond job priority (Recommendation 3 from previous list), integrate a mechanism to dynamically adjust the `CLOUD_DETERRENCE_FACTOR` based on the current and *projected congestion* of local servers of the required `server_type`. If, for instance, local servers are above a `CONGESTION_THRESHOLD` for the next few hours (based on `capacity_usage`), significantly reduce cloud deterrence to offload work and prevent cascading delays, making cloud a more proactive pressure release valve.

4.  **Enrich Objective Function with Projected Future Compute Costs**: Currently, `operation_objective` uses `remaining_duration` primarily for delay penalties. Extend this by incorporating an estimate of the *future compute costs* for the `remaining_duration` of operations of the current job. This could involve using a baseline cost (e.g., average local server cost or cloud cost if local is unavailable) to calculate an estimated future compute expenditure, providing a more comprehensive total cost for the entire job sequence to inform the current placement decision.

5.  **Implement Smart Tie-Breaking for "Best Fit" Local Server Selection**: Once the "Best Fit" local search is in place (Recommendation 2 from previous list), introduce a tie-breaking strategy for situations where multiple local slots yield the exact same minimal cost. Prioritize choices that contribute to better system health, such as: 1) selecting the slot that leaves the largest contiguous block of capacity free, 2) distributing load across different servers (e.g., pick the server with the lowest projected future utilization), or 3) preferring an earlier start time if all other factors are equal.
