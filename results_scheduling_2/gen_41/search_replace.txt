<find_best_local_slot>
Replaces the "first available slot" greedy selection for local machines with a "best available slot" search within a bounded window. This allows the scheduler to find a more optimal local placement based on the objective function, rather than just the earliest, potentially reducing overall cost or delay. The `MAX_LOCAL_SEARCH_WINDOW_HOURS` is moved to the top as a constant for clearer definition and tunability. The `break` statement after finding the first slot on a local server is removed.

This change directly implements Recommendation 1: "Replace 'First Available Slot' with Operation-Level Local Search Window". By searching for the *best* slot instead of the first, the scheduler can make more informed local resource allocation decisions, potentially leading to lower compute costs or reduced delay penalties.
