<lookahead_simulation>
Refine Lookahead Simulation with Greedy Subsequent Operation Placement

This edit implements an enhanced lookahead mechanism to improve scheduling decisions. Instead of simply summing the durations of remaining operations to estimate job completion, the scheduler now simulates a greedy placement of all *subsequent operations* for the current job. This provides a more accurate projection of the total job completion time and accumulated future compute costs, allowing the current operation's placement decision to be made with better foresight.

Key changes:
1.  **New Helper Function `_simulate_greedy_scheduling_of_remaining_ops`**: This function is introduced to perform a simplified, greedy scheduling simulation of the operations that follow the current one. It identifies the earliest available slot for each subsequent operation (first-fit on local machines within a short window, immediate on cloud) and accumulates their compute costs and total duration. It uses a *copy* of the capacity state to avoid side effects.
2.  **Modified `_calculate_placement_score`**: This function is updated to accept the `simulated_remaining_ops_cost` and `simulated_remaining_ops_duration` from the new simulation helper. It now incorporates these future costs and the more accurate projected completion time when calculating the overall score for a candidate placement of the *current* operation.
