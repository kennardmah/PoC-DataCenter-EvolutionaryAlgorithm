<local_best_fit>
For local machines, change the search heuristic from "first available slot" to "best available slot within the search window". Instead of breaking after finding the first slot, the algorithm will now iterate through all possible start times within the defined search horizon for each local machine, identifying the slot that yields the lowest objective score for that specific machine. This allows for a more optimal selection of local resources, as it considers potential future availability that might be cheaper or lead to less delay, instead of just grabbing the first spot. This refined local search is then compared against the cloud option to make the final placement decision. This improvement aims to reduce overall cost by better utilizing local resources and avoiding premature or suboptimal cloud placements, aligning with the "Refined 'Best-of-N-First-Fit' Evaluation" recommendation (where N is the entire search window for each server).
