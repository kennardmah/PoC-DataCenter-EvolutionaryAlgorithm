<local_best_fit_search>
A refined local search strategy is introduced for local machines. Instead of simply selecting the *first* available slot encountered within the search window, the scheduler will now evaluate *all* available slots within that window for a given local machine and select the one that yields the *lowest cost*. This "best-fit" approach for local machines, combined with the overall best-fit selection against cloud options, is expected to find more optimal placements. Additionally, the search range is made inclusive to ensure the entire calculated search window is considered.
