<DESCRIPTION>
Introduced `LOCAL_CONGESTION_DELAY_FACTOR` as a new tunable parameter. This factor is used to heuristically estimate additional delay for subsequent operations if the current operation is placed on a local machine. This provides a more realistic projection of a job's overall completion time when considering local versus cloud resources, nudging the scheduler towards cloud for longer jobs on local machines if congestion is expected. This directly addresses Recommendation #4 by refining the lookahead simulation in a computationally inexpensive way.
</DESCRIPTION>

apply_congestion_factor
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
Modified the `_calculate_placement_score` helper function to incorporate the `LOCAL_CONGESTION_DELAY_FACTOR`. When evaluating a local machine placement, the duration of *remaining operations* for the job is increased by this factor. This simulates the potential for future delays or queuing on local resources, making the projected completion time more accurate for local assignments compared to cloud. This refinement helps the scheduler make more informed trade-offs between immediate local cost savings and potential future delays, aligning with the "Refine Lookahead Simulation" recommendation.
</DESCRIPTION>
