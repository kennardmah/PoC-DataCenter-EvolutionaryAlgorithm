<first_fit_local_cloud_retuning>
Revert local search to first-fit per machine and tune cloud deterrence for improved online performance.
The previous iteration of the code, which implemented a "best available slot within a window" search for local machines, led to a performance regression. This edit proposes to revert the local machine search strategy to a "first available slot" heuristic. This simpler approach reduces computational complexity and can sometimes lead to better overall system flow in online scenarios by quickly utilizing available capacity.

Additionally, the cloud deterrence parameters are re-tuned to make cloud resources more readily accessible when needed:
1.  **`CLOUD_DETERRENCE_BASE` is reduced from `75.0` to `50.0`**: This lowers the penalty for using cloud machines, making them a more competitive option against local resources, especially when local capacity is tight or expensive.
2.  **`CLOUD_EMERGENCY_DISCOUNT` is increased from `0.8` to `0.9`**: This further reduces the effective cloud deterrence for `emergency` jobs, making cloud almost a no-brainer for critical tasks to prevent severe delays, aligning with the need for resilience during emergencies.

This combination aims to optimize the balance between local resource preference and the strategic use of cloud to meet deadlines and manage load, especially under unexpected circumstances.
