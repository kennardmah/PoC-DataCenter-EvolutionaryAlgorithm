<DESCRIPTION>
This edit introduces two significant enhancements to the scheduling logic to improve decision-making under contention and tight deadlines.

1.  **Slack-Aware Procrastination Penalty**: The `procrastination_penalty` within the scoring function is now non-linear. It scales exponentially based on the job's remaining slack after a potential placement. This change makes the scheduler highly sensitive to consuming the slack of jobs that are already at risk of being delayed. It heavily penalizes waiting when the job has little or no time buffer, preventing the myopic consumption of critical time on early operations and promoting a more holistic view of the job's entire lifecycle.

2.  **Robust Best-Fit Local Search**: The local search strategy has been refactored from a complex, iterative "first-fit" heuristic to a more robust "best-fit" search. The new logic evaluates all available time slots for each local server within a dynamic search horizon and identifies the single best placement (lowest score) for that server. It then compares this server's best option against the global best (including the cloud benchmark and other local servers' bests). This "best-of-best-fits" approach is more thorough, ensuring the scheduler doesn't miss superior, slightly later placements in favor of the very first available slot, leading to better cost and delay trade-offs.
</DESCRIPTION>
