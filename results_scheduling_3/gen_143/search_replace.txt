<DESCRIPTION>
This edit refines the resource contention and capacity preservation heuristics within `PlacementDecisionMaker` by unifying them into a single, more robust penalty calculation.

Key changes:
1.  **Tiered Non-Linear Load Penalty (Recommendation 1, Part 1):** The `load_penalty` is enhanced to be a tiered non-linear function. It applies a quadratic penalty for utilization exceeding a `CRITICAL_UTIL_THRESHOLD` (e.g., 70%) and a much steeper cubic penalty for utilization exceeding a `MAX_UTIL_THRESHOLD_FOR_CUBIC` (e.g., 90%). This ensures a strong disincentive for extreme congestion.
2.  **Integrated Capacity Buffer Penalty (Recommendation 1, Part 2):** The `capacity_preservation_penalty` is now directly integrated into the same calculation loop as the load penalty, simplifying the logic. It penalizes scheduling decisions that reduce the available capacity below a `SAFE_RESERVE_THRESHOLD`, with the penalty scaled by the job's urgency.
3.  **Unified Lookahead Window:** Both load and capacity preservation now use a single `combined_lookahead_window`, determined by the maximum of operation duration, hotspot lookahead, and capacity preservation lookahead. This ensures a consistent future view.
4.  **Refined Procrastination Penalty (Recommendation 2 & 5):** Minor adjustments were made to the exponential factor and urgency scaling within the "Multi-Operation Impact" section of the `procrastination_penalty`. This amplifies the penalty for critical slack shortfalls, especially for urgent jobs, proactively preserving flexibility for subsequent operations.
5.  **Normalization:** The combined `resource_contention_penalty` is now normalized by the `combined_lookahead_window` to make its magnitude more consistent across operations and instances.

This change aims to improve performance by making the scheduling decisions more acutely aware of both immediate and impending resource contention and the need to preserve capacity, especially for high-priority or multi-operation jobs, leading to more resilient and cost-effective schedules.
</DESCRIPTION>

            capacity_preservation_penalty /= self.inst.time_horizon # Normalize

                       delay_penalty * self.inst.weights['delay_penalty'] +
                       procrastination_penalty + load_penalty + capacity_preservation_penalty)
