<dynamic_urgency_and_cloud_bias>
This edit refines two key aspects of the scheduler's heuristic:
1.  **Dynamic Procrastination Penalty with Exponential Urgency Scaling**: The existing `procrastination_penalty` for waiting time is enhanced. Instead of a linear scaling with slack, it now uses an exponential increase for operations projected to be late (`current_slack_for_op <= 0`), and a quadratic increase for operations with critically low positive slack. This makes the penalty for waiting *much more aggressive* when jobs are urgent or already delayed, compelling the scheduler to find a quicker (potentially cloud) slot. The base `urgency_factor` is also directly mapped to job priorities for clearer impact.
2.  **Corrected Adaptive Cloud Fallback Threshold**: The `cloud_aggressiveness_threshold` values were inverted for their intended effect. The values have been corrected to reflect the desired behavior: higher priority jobs will now genuinely be *more willing to use cloud* even if local is slightly cheaper (requiring local to be significantly cheaper to be chosen), while lower priority jobs will *prefer local* even if it's slightly more expensive than cloud. This aligns the heuristic with the strategic goal of balancing cost vs. speed based on urgency.

These changes aim to improve the scheduler's responsiveness to critical jobs and optimize cost for less urgent ones more effectively, especially under fluctuating capacity and emergency conditions.
