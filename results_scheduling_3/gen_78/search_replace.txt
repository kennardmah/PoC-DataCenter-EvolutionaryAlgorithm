<DESCRIPTION>
This modification significantly refines the `multi-operation impact penalty` within `_calculate_operation_score`. Instead of a simple linear penalty, it introduces a non-linear (exponential) increase as the job's remaining slack approaches and falls below its total remaining duration, considering the number of remaining operations. This makes the scheduler more sensitive to cascading delays and encourages earlier completion for complex jobs with tight deadlines, particularly those with many subsequent operations. The penalty's aggressiveness is scaled by job urgency.
</DESCRIPTION>
