<DESCRIPTION>
This edit enhances the scheduling heuristic by integrating two proactive penalties into the operation scoring function:

1.  **Refined Multi-Operation Slack Preservation Penalty:** The previous `multi_op_impact_penalty` is replaced and improved to explicitly penalize if placing the current operation disproportionately consumes the available slack for subsequent operations within the *same job*. It calculates a "minimum slack buffer per future operation" and applies an exponential penalty if the job's overall remaining slack falls below the total required buffer, scaled by job urgency. This encourages maintaining a healthy temporal buffer for chained operations, especially for critical jobs.

2.  **Proactive Capacity Preservation Penalty:** A new penalty component is introduced for local servers. It discourages scheduling an operation if doing so would cause the server's *available capacity* (after accounting for currently scheduled tasks and machine failures) to drop below a predefined "safe reserve percentage" within a specified future `lookahead` window (e.g., 6 hours). This penalty is exponential, increasing sharply as the capacity dips below the reserve, and is scaled by the job's urgency. This helps to prevent resource exhaustion, maintain resilience, and ensure capacity for upcoming high-priority or emergency jobs.

These changes are inspired by Recommendation 1, aiming to make the scheduler more robust to future uncertainties and better at managing capacity and job flow by proactively preserving critical resources.
