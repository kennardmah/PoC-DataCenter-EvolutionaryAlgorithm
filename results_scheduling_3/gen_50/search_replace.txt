<priority_scaled_penalties>
Apply priority-scaled penalties directly to time-sensitive cost components.

The original code multiplied the entire `total_score` by `self.urgency_profile.criticality_factor` and then adjusted cloud costs. This edit refines the objective function by applying the `criticality_factor` directly to the `delay_cost`, `procrastination_penalty`, and `deadline_proximity_penalty` components. This ensures that urgency (driven by priority and slack) explicitly and proportionally amplifies the penalties for delays and unnecessary waiting, making the scheduler more sensitive to urgent jobs' time constraints. Compute cost, being a more fixed resource cost, is not scaled by urgency directly. The additional penalty for being late in `deadline_proximity_penalty` is also adjusted and scaled by the `criticality_factor`. The capacity hotspot penalty continues to be scaled by the criticality factor.
