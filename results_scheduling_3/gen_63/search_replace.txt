<DESCRIPTION>
This enhancement implements a dynamic adjustment for cloud aggressiveness based on the overall system load of local machines.

Key changes:
1.  **Expanded Priority Definitions**: Added 'emergency' priority to `priority_base_horizon`, `priority_procrastination_multiplier`, and `cloud_aggressiveness_threshold` with highly aggressive values to reflect its critical nature. The interpretation of `priority_load_multiplier` for emergencies was adjusted to penalize less, allowing critical jobs to "push through" congestion.
2.  **System-Wide Load Calculation (`_get_system_wide_local_load`)**: A new private method is introduced to calculate the average utilization of all local (non-cloud) servers over a future lookahead window (set to `inst.time_horizon // 2`). This provides a real-time assessment of local capacity pressure, anticipating future bottlenecks.
3.  **Adaptive Cloud Preference**: The `cloud_pref_factor` (which dictates how much cheaper local needs to be compared to cloud) is now dynamically adjusted. When the `system_load_factor` is high, `cloud_pref_factor` is reduced, making cloud options relatively more attractive. This encourages offloading to the cloud during periods of local congestion or emergencies, improving system resilience and reducing local bottlenecks, especially for higher priority jobs. The adjustment logic `(1.0 - system_load_factor * 0.5)` biases towards cloud more strongly as local load increases.
</DESCRIPTION>
