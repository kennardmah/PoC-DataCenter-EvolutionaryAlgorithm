implement_cascading_penalty
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements the cascading impact factor for the procrastination penalty, directly addressing Recommendation 5: "Incorporate Multi-Operation Impact into Procrastination Penalty."

A `cascading_impact_factor` is calculated based on:
1.  `num_future_operations`: More remaining operations mean a higher potential for cascading delays.
2.  `current_slack_for_op`: If slack is negative or very low, the impact of further delay is more severe.

This factor non-linearly increases the `procrastination_penalty`, especially for multi-step jobs nearing their deadline. This encourages scheduling operations for complex, urgent jobs more proactively to prevent compounded delays.
</DESCRIPTION>
