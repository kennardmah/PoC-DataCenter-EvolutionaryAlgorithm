<quadratic_load_hotspot>
Introduce quadratic load balancing and hotspot penalties for local servers.
</NAME>

<DESCRIPTION>
This edit implements Recommendation 1 by enhancing the `_calculate_operation_score` method within the `JobScheduler`.

1.  **Quadratic Load Balancing:** The existing load-balancing penalty, which was linear with `avg_utilization`, is modified to be quadratic (`avg_utilization ** 2`). This means that as a server approaches full utilization, the penalty for placing an operation on it increases much more steeply, strongly disincentivizing scheduling on highly contended local resources. This aligns with successful patterns observed in previous generations.

2.  **Predictive Hotspot Penalty:** A new penalty component, `hotspot_penalty`, is introduced for local servers. This penalty is applied if placing the current operation would cause the server's *remaining available capacity* (after accounting for scheduled operations and potential failures) to fall below a predefined `hotspot_threshold` (e.g., 20% of its original base capacity) at any point during the operation's execution. This proactively discourages actions that would lead to critical capacity shortages, improving resilience and resource distribution, especially under emergency scenarios. New parameters `self.hotspot_threshold` and `self.hotspot_penalty_factor` are added to the `JobScheduler` to control this behavior.
</DESCRIPTION>
