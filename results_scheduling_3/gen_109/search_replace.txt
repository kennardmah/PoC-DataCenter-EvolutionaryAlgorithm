<capacity_preservation_and_load_rebalance>
Integrating proactive multi-operation slack preservation and capacity preservation penalties, alongside non-linear load balancing with a predictive hotspot penalty. This change refines the `_calculate_operation_score` function by:
1.  **Introducing a `capacity_preservation_penalty`:** This penalty is applied if a local server's available capacity drops below a `MIN_CAPACITY_THRESHOLD` (e.g., 2 units) or a `SAFE_CAPACITY_PERCENTAGE` (e.g., 25% of total capacity) during the operation or within a short lookahead. This aims to prevent critical resource depletion and ensure some capacity remains for future high-priority jobs. The penalty scales exponentially with the shortfall and linearly with job urgency.
2.  **Enhancing the load balancing penalty:** The existing load penalty now uses a more aggressive non-linear (quadratic) function for utilization exceeding a `CRITICAL_UTILIZATION_THRESHOLD` (e.g., 80%). This makes high utilization much more costly, discouraging over-scheduling on busy local machines. The lookahead window is maintained.
3.  **Adding a `LOCAL_PREFERENCE_BONUS`:** A small, static negative bonus is added to local options to slightly favor them when other scores are very close, encouraging utilization of owned resources first.
4.  **Refactoring constants:** New constants for these penalties and thresholds are added directly into the `PlacementDecisionMaker` class for better tunability and encapsulation.

These changes are inspired by recommendations from Generations 92, 93, 94, 96, 97, 98, and 99, focusing on better resource management, preemptive bottleneck avoidance, and more nuanced cost calculation. The dynamic scaling with `urgency_score` ensures that critical jobs are less likely to cause severe resource contention.
