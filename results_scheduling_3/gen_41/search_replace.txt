<DESCRIPTION>
This edit addresses two key issues in the `JobScheduler`. First, it fixes a bug in the `_calculate_operation_score` where the `urgency_factor` for the procrastination penalty was incorrectly scaled, penalizing low-priority jobs more than high-priority ones. This is replaced with a direct `priority_procrastination_multiplier` that correctly applies aggressive penalties for waiting on critical and urgent jobs. Second, it enhances the heuristic parameter maps (`priority_base_horizon`, `cloud_aggressiveness_threshold`) to include the 'critical' priority level, making the scheduler more robust for emergency scenarios. Additionally, the slack-based penalty is intensified using exponential and quadratic scaling to make the scheduler more responsive to jobs approaching their deadlines. This creates a more logical and effective trade-off between waiting for cheap local resources and using expensive cloud resources to meet deadlines.
</DESCRIPTION>
