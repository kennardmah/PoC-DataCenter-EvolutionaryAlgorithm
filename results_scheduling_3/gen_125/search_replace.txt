<DESCRIPTION>
This edit enhances the load-balancing penalty mechanism by introducing a more robust hotspot avoidance strategy. It modifies the `PlacementDecisionMaker` to:
1.  Introduce `min_capacity_buffer_ratio` and `critical_capacity_penalty_magnitude` parameters to define a minimum buffer of available capacity that should be maintained on local servers.
2.  Refine the `_calculate_operation_score` method's load penalty to include:
    *   A stronger, non-linear (quadratic) penalty for high utilization (above 70%). The multiplier for this penalty is increased.
    *   A distinct, strong exponential penalty if scheduling an operation would cause the *remaining available capacity* (considering potential failures) to drop below the `min_capacity_buffer_ratio` at any point in the lookahead window. This proactively guards against full saturation and bottlenecks, improving resilience, especially in emergency scenarios.
3. The total load penalty is still scaled by job urgency.

This change aims to prevent local machines from becoming critically overloaded, thereby distributing load more effectively, improving system resilience, and potentially reducing overall delays and costs, particularly during periods of high demand or machine failures.
</DESCRIPTION>
