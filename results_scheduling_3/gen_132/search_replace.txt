<DESCRIPTION>
This edit refines the job urgency profile and integrates it more deeply into the load-balancing and cloud-preference decisions.

**Key Changes:**

1.  **Removed Redundant `capacity_usage` Initialization:** The explicit loop initializing `capacity_usage[server_id] = defaultdict(int)` was removed from `schedule_job_online`. The `capacity_usage` being a `defaultdict(dict)` correctly handles missing `server_id` keys, and `capacity_usage[server_id].get(t, 0)` works even if the inner dictionary is a standard `dict`. This simplifies the code without impacting functionality.

2.  **Refined `JobUrgencyProfile` Parameters:**
    *   **Cloud Aggressiveness Factor:** The calculation for `cloud_aggressiveness_factor` is made more sensitive to `urgency_score` and `system_load`. Higher urgency and higher system load will make the scheduler more aggressive about using the cloud (lower `cloud_aggressiveness_factor`), prioritizing speed and avoiding local congestion.
    *   **Local Search Horizon:** The `search_horizon` for local placements is now more sharply reduced for urgent jobs, pushing them to find a slot quickly or opt for the cloud.
    *   **Procrastination Penalty Multiplier:** The penalty for delaying an operation is made steeper for more urgent jobs.
    *   **New `load_penalty_sensitivity`:** A new attribute `load_penalty_sensitivity` is added to the urgency profile. This value scales based on job urgency, making high-priority jobs inherently more sensitive to local machine utilization and thus more likely to avoid hotspots.

3.  **Enhanced Non-Linear Load-Balancing in `_calculate_operation_score`:**
    *   The `load_penalty` now applies a penalty for utilization exceeding 50% (down from 70%).
    *   The penalty is `(current_utilization ** dynamic_exponent)`, where `dynamic_exponent` itself increases with job urgency (ranging from `~2.1` for low urgency to `4.0` for high urgency). This creates a much more aggressive and non-linear penalty for higher utilization, especially for urgent jobs.
    *   The overall `load_penalty` is then scaled by the `load_penalty_sensitivity` from the job's urgency profile, ensuring urgent jobs are significantly deterred by local congestion.
</DESCRIPTION>
